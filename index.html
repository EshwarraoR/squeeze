Squeeze

Runs in your browser

Shrink files to a size you choose and switch their format. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

No files yet.

What it can do
  • Images (PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG, AVIF) convert to PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF*, PDF, ZIP or GZ. Set a target size and the quality, then dimensions, are reduced until it fits.
  • CSV ⇄ JSON, Markdown/Text → HTML, HTML → Text, plus Markdown/Text conversions.
  • Any file can be packed into ZIP or GZ. Lossless compression has a floor, so already-compressed files (MP4, MP3, ZIP) may not shrink to your target; the result says so.
  • Video, audio, Office documents and PDF-to-image conversion need server tools and are not supported in a static page.
  • *AVIF output depends on your browser.
